DATA SOVEREIGNTY MANIFESTO

Privacy Policy & SafeAI Architecture

At Epochlypse Research, we believe that privacy is not a bureaucratic checkbox — it is a foundational architectural invariant. We build autonomous AI systems and local edge software founded on the principle that your data belongs strictly to you.

SECTION 01 // ARCHITECTURAL PRIVACY LOCAL EXECUTION

1. Zero Cloud Data Collection & Local Autonomy

All software and AI systems published by Epochlypse Research — including our flagship AutoML platform EpochGo — are engineered to be 100% local-first. When you run our software:

  • Your datasets, CSV files, and feature matrices never leave your local hardware or local Docker containers.
  • Model weights, serializations (.joblib files), and training artifacts are generated and stored exclusively on your disk.
  • We embed zero remote telemetry, tracking beacons, diagnostic phone-homes, or third-party analytics endpoints.
SECTION 02 // WEB PLATFORM NO SURVEILLANCE

2. Website Analytics, Cookies & Trackers

This website (epochlypse.com) operates under an absolute zero-surveillance design:

  • We do not set tracking cookies, marketing cookies, or third-party identifiers in your browser.
  • We do not use invasive third-party analytics suites (e.g., Google Analytics, Meta Pixel).
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your IP address, browser metadata, or visitation logs to any third party or advertising broker.
